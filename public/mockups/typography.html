Handwriting and an editorial mix

Each idea sits over a frame grabbed from the site. The signature here is a script font standing in for your real one: traced from your own handwriting it would write the same way, just more personally.

Idea 1

The signature, written on

The name writes itself stroke by stroke, with ink running from warm peach to the site's pale blue, then fills in. Tilted a few degrees as if signed by hand. Move your pointer over it: it leans like it's pinned to glass.

The editorial mix

Three voices: a script for the personal words, Instrument Serif italic for emphasis, and Satoshi (Fontshare) as a quieter sans for everything else. Hand-drawn marks pick out key words as captions reveal.
