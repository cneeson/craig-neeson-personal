Craig Neeson, set apart

Caveat, Fraunces and Instrument Serif, with the surname a different voice from the first name: lighter, italic, or a different face. All three fonts are free on Google Fonts. Fraunces is variable (weight, optical size, and "soft" and "wonky" settings), so its weight can move.

The surname relaxes

Both names arrive in bold, then the surname eases down to a light, soft, wonky weight while the first name holds. Only possible with a variable font like Fraunces, and nearly free to run. Press Replay to see it again.
